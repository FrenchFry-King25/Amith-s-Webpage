Vertical FOV side view

Robot faces right. The shaded wedge is the camera’s vertical field of view. Red is deadspace between the front of the chassis and the first ground the camera can see. Green is ground it can see.

All distances are measured along the ground from the front of the chassis. Amber is ground that falls inside the camera’s minimum stereo depth, which is measured along the viewing axis (the amber wedge at the lens shows that cutoff). Model assumes flat ground and an ideal camera. It does not account for the chassis blocking the lower part of the view, which can add deadspace when the camera sits behind the front edge and above the chassis.
